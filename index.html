About Lekha

I work between architectural systems and emotional narratives.

My graduate work at Arizona State University focused on ecological urbanism, adaptive reuse, desert climate, and the spatial performance of thresholds. I am drawn to architecture that is not just seen, but felt, through shade, air, memory, material, and community.

I also bring two years of professional practice experience from India, where I worked directly under a principal architect on high-rise residential projects, design development, and architectural coordination. Across academic, professional, and written work, I aim to communicate design clearly, beautifully, and with purpose.
