Design Philosophy

Architecture should listen before it speaks.

My work is rooted in the belief that architecture is not just an object placed on land, but a living mediator between people, climate, ecology, and memory. I am drawn to spaces that filter light, invite air, hold shade, revive water, and create a sense of belonging.

Growing up in Mumbai and studying within the desert landscape of Arizona shaped my understanding of density, heat, adaptation, and community. Through projects like ECS and Colony_518, I explore architecture as a responsive system, one that protects fragile environments while creating meaningful human thresholds.

I am interested in design that does not overpower its context, but listens to it, learns from it, and gently transforms it.
